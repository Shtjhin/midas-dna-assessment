About the MIDAS DNA Assessment

This assessment measures how well an individual aligns with Midas Talent Match's five core values (the acronym MIDAS): Mastery, Impact, Diversity, Accountability, and Service Excellence. Each value is mapped to a domain/facet of the Big Five (OCEAN) personality model — Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism (Costa & McCrae, 1992; Goldberg, 1990) — so that each statement is grounded in well-researched personality traits rather than assumptions about company culture.

Methodological note: this is a custom questionnaire that draws on the Big Five facet taxonomy; it is not a standardized instrument that has undergone formal validity/reliability testing. For high-stakes decisions (e.g. C-level positions), combine this with a validated instrument (IPIP-NEO-120, BFI-2) and a review by an industrial psychologist.

Scoring Scheme

The rating scale is a 4-point forced-choice scale (1–4, no neutral option) so respondents cannot "play it safe" by selecting the midpoint. Normal items are scored as-is (1–4). Reverse-scored items (marked R) are inverted: 5 − score. Per-value score = sum of 5 items (range 5–20). Total score = sum of 5 values (range 25–100).
